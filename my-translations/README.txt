The studio reads them from this folder:
- translations (1.0.2 and later) appear in the "English" menu on the Bible page, above the Hebrew OT,
  the Septuagint and the Greek NT;
- commentaries (1.0.5 and later) appear in the Commentary panel below the Word panel, where you can edit
  them (Edit, + Section, Delete). Each save keeps the previous version in the "backups" folder here,
  and reinstalling never replaces a commentary that is already in this folder.
- "All my books and commentaries" in that menu lists every place in all of them that cites the passage
  you are reading (click a word for one verse); click a place to read it in its chapter.
